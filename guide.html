Plan the war before the war

GW Planner is a battle board for Where Winds Meet guild wars. Build both rosters, put teams on the real map, draw the calls, and play the plan back phase by phase. It runs in your browser, works offline, and keeps everything on your device.

Screenshots updated October 14, 2026

Getting started

Open the planner and you land on an empty board with a short checklist in the bottom-right corner of the map. Each step ticks itself off as you do it, and the highlighted step has a button that does it for you.

  1. Add a team. Under Attack or Defense in a roster.
  2. Put it on the map. Drag the team's grip onto the map, or tap its pin.
  3. Plan a phase. Open the timeline, add a phase, move teams, press play.

The checklist disappears once all three steps are done. Close it with ✕ to hide it for good in this browser. Everything you do is saved automatically, so you can close the tab at any time.

The workspace

The screen has four parts.

Top bar
The strategy's name and your list of strategies, undo and redo, a link to this guide, the save status, and Share, Import and Export. The Our Guild and Enemy buttons at either end show or hide each side's roster.
Rosters
One column per side, listing its teams and players.
Battle map
The real guild war map. Scroll to zoom toward the cursor and drag empty space to pan. The tool rail runs down its left edge.
View controls
Top right of the map: zoom out, the zoom level (click it to reset to 100%), zoom in, fit to screen, export the board as a PNG, and the keyboard shortcut list.

Hover any button to see what it does and its shortcut key.

Rosters and teams

Our Guild and Enemy each hold up to 30 players. The header shows the current count, with a thin bar along its bottom edge that fills as the side fills up and turns red when you go over. Below it, two tallies count players by role (tank, DPS, healer) and by lane (top, mid, bottom).

Teams

  • Teams are grouped into Attack and Defense. Add one with the dashed button at the bottom of either group.
  • Click the name to rename a team. Leave it blank to use the automatic name, such as Att 1.
  • The bar next to the name shows how many players the team has against its target size.
  • Expand a team with the arrow to set its lane, its target size (type a number, use − +, or pick 5 or 10) and its colour.
  • Add players with + Player and set each one's role. The chips below the team count each role.
  • Yellow warnings point out gaps, such as a team with no healer or no lane.
  • The copy icon duplicates a team with its players. The ✕ deletes it, and the message that appears has an Undo button.

Teams on the map

A team goes on the map as a single pill. There are two ways to place it:

  • Drag the dotted grip next to the team's name onto the map.
  • Tap the pin on the team card. The team lands in the middle of the view, or moves there if it's already placed. This works on touch screens and from the keyboard. On narrow screens the roster closes so you can see where the team landed.

The pill's border shows its side and group: blue or teal for your Attack and Defense, red or orange for the enemy's. The badge shows how many players the team has. The label underneath shows its lane and role mix, such as Top · T2 D6 H2.

  • Drag a pill to move it.
  • Middle-click a pill to shrink it to a small disc, and again to restore it.
  • Double-click a pill to take it off the map. The team stays in the roster.

Drawing

The tool rail on the left of the map holds the drawing tools. The brass highlight shows the active tool.

Select / pan V
Select and move pieces, or drag empty space to pan.
Freehand D
Draw a free line by dragging.
Arrow A
Drag from the start point to where the arrow should point.
Curved arrow G
Drag to draw it. Once it's selected, drag the round handle to bend it, or drag the handle across the arrow to curve it the other way.
Shapes
Rectangle R, ellipse C, triangle T and straight line L. Drag to set the size.
Eraser E
Click a drawing, or drag across several, to delete them.

While a drawing tool is active, a strip next to the rail sets the colour and line width for new drawings. Press Esc to go back to the select tool.

Markers, notes and timers

The second group on the rail adds pieces to the middle of the view. Each new piece steps aside so it doesn't land on top of another.

Objective markers
Attack, Danger, Defend, Hold, Yes and No. Select one to give it a label, resize it, or rotate its point toward a target.
Notes
Sticky notes for calls and reminders. Double-click to write, pick a colour from the dots along the top, and drag the bottom-right corner to resize.
Timers
Countdowns with start, pause, reset and ±10 seconds. The duration is saved with the strategy. The running countdown is only on your screen.

Notes and timers keep the same size on screen as you zoom, and they appear in PNG and video exports.

Selecting and editing

With the select tool, click any piece to select it. A bar appears at the bottom of the map with what you can do to it.

  • Drawings: change the colour and line width.
  • Notes: Edit text.
  • Team pills: Collapse to a disc, or Take off map.
  • Everything: Duplicate (Ctrl D), Back and Forward ([ ]) to change which piece is on top, and Delete (Del).

Undo

Deleting or clearing happens straight away, and a message at the top of the screen offers Undo. The message stays while you hover over it. The undo and redo buttons in the top bar (Ctrl Z, Ctrl Shift Z) step through every change to the strategy.

The trash can at the bottom of the rail clears all team pills, markers or drawings at once. Clearing team pills only takes them off the map; the teams stay in the rosters.

Phases and animation

Phases turn the board into a plan you can play. Each phase is a snapshot of where everything is. Playing moves the pieces smoothly from one phase to the next.

  1. Click Plan phases on the rail. The timeline bar opens at the bottom of the map.
  2. Click + Phase. The new phase starts as a copy of the current one, or of the board if it's the first.
  3. With the phase selected, move teams and markers where they should be at that point. Moves only change the selected phase.
  4. Press play to watch the plan unfold.

Working with phases

  • Start is the board itself. Select it to edit the starting positions.
  • Click a phase to edit it, double-click to rename it, and drag to reorder. You can also use Move earlier / Move later, Duplicate and Delete phase.
  • Transition is how long the move into the phase takes, and hold is how long the phase stays on screen before the next one. Ease sets how the movement starts and stops.
  • Visible picks which pieces appear in the phase. While a phase is selected, Del hides a piece in that phase only.
  • Paths sends a team along a line or arrow you've drawn, instead of in a straight line.

Playback

Drag the scrubber to jump to any moment. Set the speed to 0.5×, 1× or 2×, and turn on loop to repeat. The trail button shows a dashed line behind each moving team. With the timeline open, Space plays and pauses, ← → step between phases, and Home goes back to Start.

The film button records one run of the plan and saves it as a video (WebM, or MP4 in browsers that can't record WebM) to share with your guild.

Your strategies

Keep as many strategies as you like, for different maps, opponents or backup plans. Click the number next to the strategy's name to see them all.

  • Click a strategy to switch to it, or + New strategy to start a fresh one.
  • Hover a strategy to rename it (or double-click its name), duplicate it, or delete it. Deleting offers Undo.
  • Rename the open strategy by clicking its name in the top bar.

Undo history belongs to each strategy and starts fresh when you switch.

Sharing and exports

Share
Copies a link that contains the whole strategy. Opening the link adds a copy to the other person's planner. Nothing is uploaded: the strategy is compressed into the link itself. If your browser blocks the clipboard, the link appears in a message for you to copy.
Export Ctrl S
Saves the strategy as a .json file, which is good for backups.
Import
Opens a .json file as a new strategy. Nothing gets overwritten.
PNG
The image button in the view controls saves the whole board as a picture, at full map size whatever your zoom.
Video
The film button in the phases bar records the animation (see Phases).

Editing the map layout

The map's fixed pieces (towers, geese, trees and the boss) can be moved if a game update changes them. Click Edit map layout on the rail.

  • Drag fixtures on the map. Select one in the list to change its size or rotation.
  • Add more fixtures from the menu at the top, or reset to the default layout (with Undo).
  • Changes are saved in this browser. Copy layout JSON or Download lets you send the layout to whoever maintains the planner, so it can become the default for everyone.

Click Done to return to planning.

Phones and tablets

  • On narrower screens the rosters slide over the map. Open them with the side buttons in the top bar; only one is open at a time.
  • Use the pin on a team card to place teams, since dragging from the roster needs a mouse.
  • Zoom with the view controls at the top right of the map, and drag to pan.
  • Buttons get bigger on touch screens so they're easier to hit.

Offline, install and updates

  • After your first visit the planner works with no internet. Your browser can also install it as an app (look for Install in the address bar or browser menu).
  • Everything is saved in this browser on this device. The dot next to Autosaves locally flashes green each time a change is saved.
  • When a new version is available, a message asks you to reload. Choose Later to finish what you're doing first.

Questions

Where are my strategies stored?

In your browser's storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing site data for the planner deletes them, so export important strategies as files.

How do I move my strategies to another computer?

Export each one (Ctrl S) and import the files there, or send yourself a Share link and open it on the other computer.

A share link says it's broken or incomplete.

The link was probably cut off when it was pasted, which some chat apps do with long links. Ask for a new link, or for the exported file.

Does the other person see my timers counting down?

No. The duration is part of the strategy, but each person starts and stops their own countdown.

The video button is greyed out or recording fails.

Add at least one phase first, and stop playback before recording. Some browsers can't record the board; Chrome and Edge can.
